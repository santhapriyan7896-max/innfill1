✦ The InnFill Process

How InnFill Works

From signup to delivery — a transparent, secure, and seamless journey for freelancers and clients alike.

✦ Full Workflow

The Complete Journey

Every step from registration to payment, explained clearly.

01

📝 Register & Set Up Your Profile

Sign up as a freelancer or client in under 2 minutes. Freelancers build a portfolio, list skills, and set availability. Clients describe what kind of work they typically hire for.

02

🔍 Discover Opportunities

Clients post projects with a description, budget, and deadline. Freelancers browse the project board or get AI-matched suggestions based on their skills and ratings. Clients can also browse freelancer profiles directly.

03

📨 Submit & Review Proposals

Freelancers submit proposals outlining their approach, timeline, and price. Clients review all proposals in one dashboard, compare profiles and ratings, and select their preferred freelancer.

04

🔐 Fund Escrow & Begin Work

Once a freelancer is selected, the client deposits the agreed amount into InnFill's Razorpay-powered escrow. Funds are secured. The freelancer is notified and work begins.

05

💬 Collaborate in Real-Time

Use the built-in chat to communicate, share files, and track milestone progress. Both parties stay informed throughout the project lifecycle with real-time notifications.

06

📦 Deliver & Request Delivery

When the work is ready, the freelancer marks the project as delivered and shares the final files. The client enters a review period (typically 7 days) to evaluate the work.

07

✅ Approve & Release Payment

If the client is satisfied, they approve the delivery and funds are released from escrow to the freelancer (minus the 14% platform commission + GST). Both parties leave ratings and reviews.

✦ Policies

Fair & Transparent Rules

We protect everyone on the platform with clear, fair policies.

🔄

Cancellation Before Work Starts

Full escrow refund to the client if cancelled before the freelancer begins. No questions asked.

⚖️

Dispute Resolution

If delivery is disputed, InnFill mediates fairly using chat history, file submissions, and project milestones.

🕐

Auto-Approval

If a client doesn't respond within 7 days of delivery, payment is automatically released to the freelancer.

🛡️

Refund Policy

Partial or full refunds may be issued based on dispute outcomes. All refunds go back to the original payment method.

📋

Revision Policy

Clients can request revisions within the agreed project scope. Unlimited revisions must be agreed beforehand.

🚫

Off-Platform Payments

All payments must go through InnFill's escrow. Off-platform payments violate our terms and void all protections.
