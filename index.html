✦ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How does InnFill's payment protection work?
When a client hires a freelancer, the agreed project amount is deposited into an escrow account powered by Razorpay. The funds are only released to the freelancer after the client reviews and approves the delivered work. This protects both parties — freelancers know they'll get paid, and clients know they'll only pay for completed, approved work.
What is InnFill's platform fee?
InnFill charges a 14% platform commission on the total project value, plus 18% GST on the commission amount. For example, on a ₹10,000 project: the commission is ₹1,400, GST is ₹252, and the freelancer receives ₹8,348. See our Pricing page for a full breakdown.
Can I cancel a project after it starts?
Yes. Either party can request cancellation. If cancelled before the freelancer begins work, the full escrow amount is refunded to the client. If work has started, the refund is determined by our dispute resolution process. See the How It Works page for full cancellation policies.
How quickly can I find a freelancer for my project?
Most projects receive their first proposals within a few hours of posting. You can also browse freelancer profiles directly and send invitations. For urgent projects, use our priority matching feature.
Is InnFill free to join?
Yes — creating an account on InnFill is completely free for both freelancers and clients. You only pay the platform fee when a project is successfully completed.
