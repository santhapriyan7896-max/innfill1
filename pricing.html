Pricing FAQs

When is the commission charged?
The commission is deducted automatically when the client releases payment from escrow. There are no upfront fees to browse or bid.
Does the client pay extra on top of 14%?
No. The 14% commission (plus GST) is deducted from the freelancer's payment. Clients pay exactly the agreed project amount — nothing more.
Is the GST included in the 14%?
No. GST (18%) is applied separately on top of the 14% commission as per Indian tax regulations. So the total deduction is 14% + (18% of 14%) = 16.52% of the project value.
